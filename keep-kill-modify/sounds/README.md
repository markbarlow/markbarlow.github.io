### Why it matters

Freesound licences are per-sound, not per-site:

- **CC0** — nothing required. Crediting anyway costs nothing.
- **CC BY** — attribution required: author, title, licence and a link. The page
  currently credits Freesound.org only, which is not sufficient on its own.
- **CC BY-NC** — non-commercial only. An away day at a company is not clearly
  non-commercial, so a sound under this licence should be replaced rather than
  argued about.
- **Sampling+** — restrictive; treat as unusable here.

If `qubodup` turns out to be the author of any of these, their profile states
the attribution they want: the name "qubodup" in a credits section.

Until the URLs are recorded, the attribution on the page is incomplete.
